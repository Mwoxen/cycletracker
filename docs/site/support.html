Frequently asked questions

How does my partner get my data? Open "Share with partner" in Settings or on Home, and let your partner scan the QR code, or send the link via AirDrop or Messages.

I got a new phone. Install the app and choose "Restore from iCloud" on the welcome screen if iCloud backup was on. Otherwise import an exported backup file.

The prediction is off. It is an estimate based on averages. Log every period start and it improves. The cycle can shift with stress, illness and travel.

How do I delete everything? Settings → Delete all data. Turn off iCloud backup before deleting to remove the backup too.
