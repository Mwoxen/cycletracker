Cycle Tracker is made for couples. The person with the cycle logs it. The partner follows along and learns, every day for a whole year, what is happening and what they can concretely do to help.

LEARN EVERY DAY
• One card a day (80-150 words) with one concrete action: say a sentence, take a task, notice something.
• One read a week under five minutes, ending with a question you can talk about together.
• One wrap-up a month with a short quiz where the answers are about what helps most.
• 12 monthly themes: the phases, menstruation, ovulation, the luteal phase, PMS and PMDD, pain, food and exercise, communication, fertility and contraception, when something is off, and life stages.

FOLLOW ALONG
• See which phase your partner is in today, the cycle day and the expected next period.
• Calendar with logged and predicted periods, fertile window and PMS window.
• Log periods, symptoms, mood, energy and notes in seconds.
• Personal overview per phase: what she logs most often, and what has helped.
• Reminders: today's card, period in two days, PMS window starting.

PRIVATE
• Everything stays on the phone. No account, no server, no analytics.
• Optional backup to your own iCloud so you can switch phones without losing anything.
• Share with your partner via QR code or link. Only when you choose to.

Danish and English.

The app is educational, not medical advice. Predictions are estimates based on averages and must not be used as contraception.
